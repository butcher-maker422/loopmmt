00The graph idea~2016

Before the Minecraft computer, an older idea: a graph database of everything worth knowing — people, places, things, ideas — wired by the relationships between them, crawled and kept current, then queried for what no ordinary collection of facts could answer. Pitched here for the marijuana newsphere. The concept that would, years later, become Loop MMT.
