Loop AI

Loop AI is a better way to AI.

You run crews of AI agents on your actual work — set them up once, name them, run them again whenever.

  • A crew is a set of AI agents you point at a job.
  • You watch them work, then run the same crew again whenever you need it.
  • The intelligence underneath is stock Claude or OpenAI, running on your own API key. You bring the brain; Loop AI is the cockpit.

The tiers below buy you two things, in order: how much crew you can run and watch at once, and then whether your crew can reach into your real apps. They never change how smart your crew is — that’s your key, your model, your call.
