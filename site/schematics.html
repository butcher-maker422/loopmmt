Schematics

The compiler’s proofs. Each one reads a batch of gifts and lays out how they wire into a whole app — which stages feed which, and which run in parallel.

Most of these carry declared gaps — and that’s the honest part. A ◈ Declared gaps verdict means the app is constructible: the wiring holds, and the missing pieces are named out loud (honest derive-first work), not swept under a green check. A ◆ Compiles complete verdict means every stage is witnessed by a shipped gift. The gifts are real and free; the wiring is a plan you can read, check, and build.
