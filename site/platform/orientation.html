What the Platform is

A growing set of composable tools and the recipes that stitch them together. What sits in the catalog today are Schematics — an app's authored declaration of the primitives it names, each proven constructible. A constructible Schematic is not a running app; it's the honest claim that the parts exist and compose, with every gap declared rather than hidden. We label a specimen a specimen — here and everywhere.

Why you can trust what you read

Every count, every row, every "what's new" line on this hub is a fold over a source-of-truth manifest, re-derived on each build. Nothing is hand-listed, so the page can't quietly drift from the collection. When a rendered page and its source disagree, the source wins — and a gate refuses to ship a page that drifted.
