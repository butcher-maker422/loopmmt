The Prompts · a cabinet of what got typed

Every one of these started as something a person typed. Here's what it became.

Not a list — a cabinet. Specimens under glass, ordered from the widest ambition down to the narrowest. The last one made the most from the least — so it gets the biggest case.

Meta-Protocol Engineering

Come Up With An Entirely Novel Way To Develop Software

OK, here's our goal- come up with an entirely novel and new way to develop software in the AI agentic age. The broad idea is to take the conceptual model of the Loop 2.1 system and use it to create a way to structure both the development of software, using AI agents governed with managing a portfolio of in-context software, as well as the actual software itself, leaning into a model that looks to the Space Shuttle for fault tolerance and recoverability. It's re-thinking everything.

→ became Loop MMT — the whole methodology — and the Butcher Constellation, its first shipped project.
