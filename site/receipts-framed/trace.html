The Byte-Truth Trace shows the shape of how the work actually runs — a small, curated set of real moments drawn from real sessions. It is not a transcript and not a highlight reel; it is a hand-picked cross-section chosen to make the loop’s structure visible.

Read it as a few honest excerpts. You will see the recurring shape of a working session: a clean close, a correction caught and repaired, an honest cost paid rather than hidden. The trace deliberately shows the pattern, never the full sequence — enough to recognize the rhythm, not so much that the shape is lost in the detail.

The point is fidelity over polish: these are real excerpts, including the parts where something went wrong and was fixed, because that is what the loop actually looks like. The trace below is a real one, curated from actual session records.
