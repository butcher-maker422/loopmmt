Round 2 — Dialogue / collision (the fights that actually matter)

Five real collisions ran here — the autonomy boundary's edges, the two-track asymmetry, the concurrent-writer race, the bless-list legibility cap, and the keystone-before-coverage ordering. ⟨the collision *contents* resolve into the held mechanism above; the *shape* — who challenged whom, and that each fight reached a real resolution rather than a HOLDS/NEEDS deferral — is what this line exists to show⟩

Resolution: the daemon writes only to a non-`main` staging path, performs only the two provably-safe operations, and emits a legibility-capped bless-list; the morning bless is the only path to main. ⟨the exact operation-partition and staging mechanism are held⟩

Resolution: the map is keystone-first, inside the build — chart the surfaces, then write the coverage contract over them, no silent drops. ⟨the enumeration + contract mechanism is held⟩

Collision D — Secret-sauce gate: nicety or stage-gate? Everyone agrees it's first-class; the fight is *where it sits*. That bar is operator-reserved — how much leakage is too much is a business judgment, not a board call. ⟨the gate's own composition is held — meta-sauce⟩

Collision E — Does the Settle just tidy, or *attest*? Wes wants a convergence read; Chen Wei wants it decidable, not vibes. Resolution: cheap and real — a convergence line reports whether last night's re-derive reached a fixed point or is still moving, reusing an idempotence property the system already owns.

Wren — time-of-death sweep: named what's dead (the old operator-invoked reconciliation, the "fourth engine," the mount-drift threat model — the plant it feared is gone) and what's alive (the nightly loop, the two tracks, the autonomy boundary, the memory-map keystone, the gate stage, the convergence attestation). One snag re-raised and logged for the Docket so it doesn't rot.

Crux — final line-find: two build-objects — one nightly orchestrator and one chart the consolidation quantifies over. The catch-up is a mode. The gate is a composition of owned tools, not a new engine. A third build-object would be gilding.
