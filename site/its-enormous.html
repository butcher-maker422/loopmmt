A note on scale

It's Enormous

Before you dig in, one honest thing about the size of what you're looking at — and how it's meant to be read.

This project is ENORMOUS. Like, you just won't believe how vastly, hugely, mind-bogglingly big it is. I mean, you may think it's a long way down the road to the chemist's, but that's just peanuts to this. With apologies to Douglas Adams.

But seriously — the entirety of LoopMMT is too big for any one person to fully parse in a short sitting. Or even one long sitting. Heck, it took me a month to really even understand what I was building when I started building it.

So this website is designed to allow two things to happen:

  1. For a human reader to open it up and see that “there is something here.”
  2. For that human reader to then have their AI system look at the website, parse it, analyze it, and report back on what it is and what we have built.

It's all there — laid out in the open for everyone to see. It's just enormous — it's like asking everyone to read all of Stephen King's books real quick. You can't do it. But your AI can.

That's how we built this website.

The doors your AI reads through

This isn't a claim — it's wired. The site publishes a machine-readable digest at its root, built for exactly this: hand it to an AI and let it read the whole thing.

/llms.txt — the plain-text map of the site, written for a language model to follow.

/index.json — the structured index of what's here, for a machine to walk.

Paste one to your AI, point it at loopmmt.com, and ask it what this is.
