The Glossary

The methodology's controlled vocabulary — the words it agreed to mean one thing. It grows by accretion, a term at a time. It's on its 57th version now, but only the versions from v31 onward live in the byte-truth repo; the earlier ones predate it. So this strip starts where the record starts, and marks the milestones the bytes can date.
