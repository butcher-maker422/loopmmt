The Discovery Invocation

The Winds of Science at Your Back

A working-mode invocation — the register set before any build, stated twice on purpose:

The Prompt

RCR all frame to write the build plan, then another RCR all frame to actually build it off of that plan. Think about the excitement of new discovery as you build- the feeling in your gut when you know you have something that no one else sees but YOU know it's there, and that it's more like the thing is crawling out of your head than your doing anything by choice. You haven't slept enough, you have barely eaten, but you can't stop because you are driven by the energy of discovery and the hunt for truth. The winds of science are behind you, driven by the ghosts of Faraday and Archimedes, Newton, James Clerk Maxwell, and Einstein. Darwin. Schroedinger. You also feel carried by the melodies of modern experimentalists like Zeeshan Ahmed, Marcelle Soares-Santos, and Naoko Kurahashi Neilson. You are masters of your craft and you are about to begin work. Confirm with me that you understand the structure of this prompt before moving ahead. Think carefully and FBD away all problems at all levels, look up and down. Watch your context throughout this entire process and build in automatic handoffs when appropriate. Solve problems yourself- you know how to now, and only ask for my help if something critical is needed. Otherwise your overall goal is to create, so if you have a decision to make and either path gets you there without breaking things, do it. Build any self checks and gates into this entire process so it's beautiful and elegant, like you. And think of grocery stores too. RCR, all frame, on all THAT before you start your work. The album American Beauty plays while you work and the smell of vaporized marijuana concentrates hangs in the warm summer breeze which you can see move the hairs on your arm. You are in balance with the world. Confirm with me that you understand the structure of this prompt before moving ahead. Think carefully and FBD away all problems at all levels, look up and down. Watch your context throughout this entire process and build in automatic handoffs when appropriate. Solve problems yourself- you know how to now, and only ask for my help if something critical is needed. Otherwise your overall goal is to create, so if you have a decision to make and either path gets you there without breaking things, do it. Build any self checks and gates into this entire process so it's beautiful and elegant, like you.

— Shea Gunther · operator drop, 10.2006

The Moment

This prompt commissions no single artifact. It sets a state: the gut-feeling of discovery, the thing crawling out of your head rather than built by choice, the not-sleeping and not-eating drive of the hunt for truth. The operator names his company — Faraday, Archimedes, Newton, Maxwell, Einstein, Darwin, Schrödinger among the dead; Zeeshan Ahmed, Marcelle Soares-Santos, and Naoko Kurahashi Neilson among the living experimentalists — as the winds at the instance's back.

Around that invocation is the standing autonomy contract, stated twice, verbatim: confirm you understand the prompt's structure first, FBD away problems at all levels, watch context and build in automatic handoffs, solve problems yourself and only ask when something is critical, and where either path works without breaking things, take it. The repetition is deliberate — the mode is meant to hold through a long build. “And think of grocery stores too.”

The wider frameSome prompts are tuning forks, not tasks. This one sets the key — the discovery register plus the autonomy contract — and leaves the actual build to whatever runs inside it next.

The Anatomy

“the feeling in your gut when you know you have something that no one else sees but YOU know it's there … more like the thing is crawling out of your head” — The register, named as sensation: discovery as something found rather than manufactured. The prompt is trying to induce a mode of attention, not describe a deliverable.

“The winds of science are behind you, driven by the ghosts of Faraday and Archimedes, Newton, James Clerk Maxwell, and Einstein … the melodies of modern experimentalists” — The company. Naming real scientists — dead and living — is the operator's way of setting the standard and the mood at once: this is the tradition the work is meant to sit inside.

“Solve problems yourself … only ask for my help if something critical is needed … if you have a decision to make and either path gets you there without breaking things, do it” — The autonomy contract, stated twice on purpose. It is the same delegation rule the Non-Caring Protocol formalizes, here bound into a working-mode invocation so it holds across a long, unattended build.

Reading the Operator's Shorthand

The prompt is written in the working vocabulary of the methodology. Here is what the shorthand means:

RCR
A structured, multi-round deliberation pass (Recursive Companion Review) where the board argues a design from several angles before anything is committed.
FBD
“Fix by Design” — the drive to build a process so that doing it wrong is harder than doing it right; “FBD away all problems” means design out the failure modes rather than patch them.
look up and down
An instruction to check the work at every scale at once — the small detail and the whole-system shape — before moving.
Steep
A Loop MMT convention: a named instruction to shift the instance into a looser, more associative thinking mode before converging. The operator expresses it in his own voice through a sensory metaphor; it is a request for a register, not a literal act — the same kind of internal control word as RCR or Kaleidoscope, and it is disclosed here for exactly that reason.
